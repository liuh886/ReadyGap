# Product Charter — ReadyGap v0.1

## Purpose

ReadyGap is a propagation-first open assessment website backed by an inspectable, gradually validated measurement method. It addresses a specific gap: available AI capability can grow while an *entity's capacity to use it effectively and responsibly* remains constrained. An entity can be an individual, team, department, enterprise, university or other coordinated actor.

**Primary user question:** "How ready am I / is my organization, which of our relevant activities can actually use AI, and what change should we make next?"

### Product outputs

1. **ReadyScore:** six-domain entity-capability profile, with provisional composite only when sufficient indicators are usable.
2. **Ready Coverage:** task-level *confirmed pilot-ready* share over a documented eligible task sample, not a claim that AI is already deployed at scale.
3. **Bottlenecks / Unlock Next:** which necessary activity-level conditions block a trial; which single changes would remove the last known pre-trial blocker.
4. **Future, separate:** measured net time, quality, costs, risk and actual business value.

### Explicit non-goals (v0.1)

- Not model benchmarking, AI intelligence ranking or a certification of production safety.
- Not a global percentile or a cross-person/company ranking.
- Not a claim of causality or ROI prediction.
- Not a replacement for security, privacy, legal or sector-specific review.
- Not a recommendation to automate tasks that should remain human-led.

### Design decisions

- **Universal construct; adapted examples:** identical underlying function, not identical organizational structure.
- **Evidence over attitude:** count with explicit numerator, denominator, time window and supporting records; task-level gate checks.
- **Two measurement units:** entity-level capability observations and independently entered task-level gate observations. Never compute both scores from one set of question answers.
- **Non-compensation:** a missing authorization or verification gate cannot be offset by a high average.
- **Fair comparisons:** report scale version, subject category, sampled scope and evidence mode. Cross-type comparability requires empirical validation.
- **Privacy-first:** no account, no cloud upload and no telemetry in the prototype.

### Public language

ReadyGap — *Know your readiness. Unlock your potential.*

Formal research umbrella: Universal AI Readiness Index (U-ARI). A public-facing experimental score is **not yet** a validated U-ARI international benchmark.
