# Validation Plan — How ReadyGap could earn its claims

The MVP is a **candidate instrument**. Numerical outputs are deterministic but that alone is not scientific validity.

## Sequence

1. **Content validity:** independently review all 12 indicator contracts with people from personal, team, public and private organizational settings. Identify irrelevant or missing capabilities.
2. **Cognitive interviews:** test whether respondents correctly understand numerator, denominator, eligibility, N/A, evidence type and gate definitions. Revise confusing wording before collecting benchmark data.
3. **Reliability:** repeat a subset after 2–4 weeks with no material process change; compare self-report with documentary/observational checks; use agreement metrics appropriate for counts and binary gates.
4. **Cross-entity fairness:** test response processes for people, teams and institutions, sectors, languages, and high/low digital maturity. Do not presume that equal numeric scores imply equivalent capacity. Audit differential item functioning and sampling biases.
5. **Prospective validity:** at baseline, collect scores plus a simpler maturity-checklist comparator. Observe 30–90 day subsequent pilot-start, reliable task completion, adverse events, net time/cost and quality. Compare incremental predictive value adjusting for task difficulty, baseline digital maturity, entity scale, model access and existing adoption.
6. **Decision utility:** independently allocate or match users to checklist-only vs ReadyGap prioritization; track whether advice changes the selected intervention and downstream verifiable results.
7. **Sensitivity:** vary weights, missingness thresholds, sample-selection rules and gate definitions. Publish how much the score, readiness classifications and priorities change.

## Explicit falsification / stop rules

- A domain consistently fails relevance or cross-type comprehension checks: redesign it.
- Coverage cannot be verified or fails inter-rater reproducibility: restrict to self-reported estimate.
- Correlation between readiness and later use disappears after controlling baseline digital maturity / prior adoption: no independent predictive validity claim.
- ReadyGap prioritization does not outperform simpler checklists on decision or outcome metrics: do not claim superior effectiveness.
- High denominator or task selection manipulation changes score dramatically: revise sampling and reporting before ranking.

## Responsible public release

Before evidence is sufficient: label **experimental, self-reported, scope-bound, no certified readiness, no percentiles**. Publish methods, changelog and aggregate sensitivity. Store no identified company workflows by default. Release any benchmark dataset only after consent and privacy review.
